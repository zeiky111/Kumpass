Finger-Spelling Data Collector

Record real FSL hand-shape samples (A–Z) using MediaPipe hand landmarks, then export CSV for training the fingerspelling classifier.

Current Label: A Total Rows: 0 Hand: -

Keyboard shortcuts: press A–Z to pick a letter, Space to capture one sample, B to capture a burst of 25.

Tip: record 60–100 samples per letter, varying hand angle, distance, and lighting a little each time. Recording can be split across multiple sessions — download a CSV now, and later import it here to merge with new captures before exporting the combined file. Once you have a CSV covering all 26 letters, place it in backend/datasets/fingerspelling/ and run python backend/scripts/train_fingerspelling_model.py to train and deploy the model.
