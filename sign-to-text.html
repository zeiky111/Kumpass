💡 Tips for Best Recognition

💡

Good Lighting

Ensure adequate lighting so the camera can clearly see your hand movements

📐

Clear View

Keep your hands visible and within the camera frame throughout signing

⚡

Deliberate Movements

Make clear, intentional hand movements at a moderate pace

📍

Hand Position

Keep hands at chest level, about 1-2 feet away from the camera

🔄

Repeat Practice

Practice the same signs multiple times to improve recognition accuracy

🎯

One Sign at a Time

Perform signs one at a time with clear pauses between them
